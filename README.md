 Fixmate – Home Service Marketplace

Fixmate is a full-stack home service marketplace application designed to connect customers with skilled service providers. It helps users find and access essential home services conveniently at their doorstep.

The platform provides a convenient way to discover services such as plumbing, electrical work, carpentry, cleaning, painting, AC repair, and appliance repair.
